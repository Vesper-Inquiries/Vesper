Common Questions
What kinds of questions can I ask?
Anything you are genuinely curious about. Vesper researches and provides information on virtually any topic. We do not provide professional legal, medical, or financial advice and nothing in our responses should be treated as such. For matters requiring licensed professional guidance, consult the appropriate professional. Requests that facilitate direct harm to others will be declined at Vesper's sole discretion. No explanation will be given.
How long does it take?
Surface questions are answered within 24 hours. Deep questions within 24 to 48 hours. Obscure questions within 48 hours. All turnaround times begin after payment is confirmed.
How do I pay?
After submitting your question you will receive a payment link via PayPal. Your question enters the queue once payment is received. Surface — $1.99 · Deep — $4.99 · Obscure — $9.99.
Is my question confidential?
Yes. Questions are not shared, published, or disclosed to third parties. Vesper does not ask why you are asking and does not retain that information beyond what is necessary to answer your question.
What if I am not satisfied?
Vesper makes every effort to provide accurate, useful information. All research is provided as-is. Due to the nature of information services, refunds are not guaranteed but concerns can be raised directly at askvesper@proton.me and will be handled personally.
Will I receive a confirmation?
Yes. An automatic confirmation is sent to your email upon submission. If you do not receive it within a few minutes check your spam folder or contact askvesper@proton.me.
